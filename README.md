## Why Computtite?

Most IT Asset Management (ITAM) tools force you into an uncomfortable choice: pay **$30–$80 per seat every month** for bloated enterprise software, or suffer with fragile, unmaintainable spreadsheets.

**Computtite is built differently:**

1. **Zero Paywalls, Zero Subscriptions** — Every feature (custom schemas, governance, reporting, plugins, cloud sync) is completely free to use.
2. **Offline-First & Truly Private** — Create local workspaces backed by fast SQLite databases on your disk. Your data never touches the internet unless you choose cloud sync.
3. **Native Desktop Speed** — Built with Tauri v2 and Rust for lightweight resource consumption, instant startup, and zero Electron bloat.
4. **Team Collaboration Ready** — When you need multi-user collaboration, connect a Supabase cloud workspace for real-time synchronization and role-based permissions.

## Key Features

### 💻 Dynamic Inventory & Schema Builder
- **Custom Asset Types:** Build custom schemas for laptops, servers, monitors, software licenses, company vehicles, and peripherals.
- **Rich Data Fields:** Text, numbers, custom select dropdowns, currency, serial numbers, warranty dates, and document attachments.
- **Relational History:** Full audit logs tracing every assignment, return, repair, and status change over time.

### 🧩 First-Party Plugin Ecosystem
- **Plugin Store:** Enable modular capabilities with granular permission checks.
- **QR & Barcode Generator:** Generate ISO/IEC 18004 QR codes and Code-128 linear barcodes with asset UUIDs for instant physical scanning.
- **Custom Visual Layouts:** Switch asset detail views between Standard, Bento Grid, and Compact cards.
- **One-Click Clipboard:** Instantly copy identifiers, serials, and custom fields.
- **Executive Stats PDF Export:** Generate professional PDF reports directly from the analytical dashboard.

### 📊 Real-Time Analytics & KPI Hub
- **Financial & Inventory KPIs:** Live metrics on total valuation, active deployment rates, maintenance queues, and scrap counts.
- **Interactive Breakdown Charts:** Visual distribution charts by category, status, and department.

### 👥 Governance & Team Permissions
- **Multi-Workspace Architecture:** Create separate, isolated environments for distinct clients, projects, or branches.
- **Role-Based Security:** Owner, Administrator, Member, and Viewer roles.
- **Custom Permission Profiles & Asset Visibility:** Restrict sensitive assets (e.g. executive hardware, master server keys) to specific individuals or teams.

### 🔄 Integrations & Export Engine
- **Cloud & Native Integrations:** Push reports directly to **Google Sheets** or **Notion Databases** via secure OAuth.
- **Multi-Format Export:** Export filtered inventory to styled Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV, or JSON with custom column templates.

## Get Started in 60 Seconds

1. **Install Computtite:** Download and run the installer for your OS.
2. **Launch Forge Wizard:** Use the built-in wizard to start with a pre-configured workspace template (IT Department, Education, Corporate, or Blank).
3. **Add Your First Asset:** Define your fields, scan or enter your serials, and organize by employee and department.
4. **Stay Updated Automatically:** Computtite automatically checks for signed updates via this repository. You can choose between **Stable** and **Beta** update channels in **Settings → Updates**.
